Map basis: Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection centred on the archipelago, drawn from Natural Earth 10 m boundaries. The frame is the Maldives plus 100 km on every side (about 71.8 to 74.7 degrees east and 1.6 degrees south to 8.0 degrees north). The Maldives is shaded. Nothing outside the archipelago is plotted, and neighbouring land is not drawn. The Maldives has no nuclear power plant, nuclear storage site, missile base, space-launch site or submarine base in the sources reviewed; none is plotted. Most positions are approximate; each tooltip gives its source and whether it is verified in this build. Reported events and claims are attributed. Events reflect reports up to 10 October 2026.

Sources: Ransomware.live (leak-site claims - group claims, not verified facts); Telegram threat-intel channels; and dark-web RSS sources reached through Tor that you verify yourself (data/onion_sources.json).
To read onion sources you need Tor on this PC: open Tor Browser (it listens on port 9150) or install and start the Tor service (port 9050). Keep it running, then press Refresh. Nothing is needed for the clear-web and Telegram sources. This dashboard never opens .onion pages in a browser and does not search or crawl the dark web.
