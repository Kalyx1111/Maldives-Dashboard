# Maldives Dashboard

**Locally Hosted OSINT & Geospatial Intelligence Platform**

Maldives Dashboard is a locally hosted OSINT platform for mapping defence infrastructure, strategic facilities, maritime routes, ports, energy assets, subsea cables, cyber institutions and regional developments relating to the Maldives.

***

#### Key Features

- **43 map points:** 26 sourced intelligence points and 17 civil airfields.
- **15 intelligence panels** covering defence, maritime, cyber, economic and regional developments.
- **Interactive map** covering the Maldives and approximately 100 km of surrounding area.
- **Strategic infrastructure** covering military establishments, airports, ports, fuel facilities and utilities.
- **Maritime intelligence** featuring the Eight Degree Channel and selected shipping-related locations.
- **Cyber intelligence** covering national cybersecurity institutions and subsea cable infrastructure.
- **Local operation** through a Windows batch launcher.

***

#### Geographic Coverage

- Maldives archipelago and approximately 100 km surrounding frame.
- Geographic bounds: 71.78°E–74.65°E and 1.59°S–8.01°N.
- Map canvas: 1,200 × 3,954 pixels.
- The Maldives is the only shaded territory.

***

#### Intelligence Categories

The dashboard includes 15 panels covering:

1. Defence & Military Infrastructure
2. Maritime Security & Shipping Routes
3. Strategic Facilities & Foreign-linked Projects
4. Ports & Commercial Infrastructure
5. Aviation & Civil Airfields
6. Energy & Fuel Infrastructure
7. Water & Waste Management
8. Subsea Cables & Connectivity
9. Cybersecurity & Threat Intelligence
10. Geopolitical & Diplomatic Developments
11. Economic & Trade Developments
12. Regional Strategic Developments
13. Environmental & Natural Risks
14. Technology & Infrastructure Projects
15. OSINT News & Reference Information

***

#### Strategic Infrastructure

- **Defence:** MNDF headquarters, regional headquarters, Coast Guard references and selected Air Corps facilities.
- **Aviation:** 17 civil airfields, including Velana, Gan, Maafaru and Kadhdhoo.
- **Ports & Maritime:** Malé Commercial Harbour, Thilafushi port relocation and the Eight Degree Channel.
- **Energy & Utilities:** Fuel storage, water infrastructure and planned waste-to-energy facilities.
- **Connectivity:** SEA-ME-WE 6 subsea cable landing and related infrastructure.
- **Cybersecurity:** National Cyber Security Agency and selected cyber references.
- **Regional Projects:** Selected Chinese-linked infrastructure and research-vessel visits, with status qualifications.

***

#### Installation & Launch

1. Extract `maldives-dashboard-v1.zip`.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Run `start.bat`.
4. Open the dashboard in your browser.

**Local URL:** `http://127.0.0.1:8840`

***

#### Testing & Limitations

- **39 Python tests passed.**
- **3 browser test suites passed.**
- The packaged ZIP was rebuilt and verified.
- The live server started without errors.
- 19 points are flagged as approximate, unverified or planned.
- Live news feeds were not tested because the build environment had no internet access.
- Maldives social-source lists are empty because no channels or accounts were verified.
- No nuclear power plants, nuclear storage facilities, missile bases, space-launch sites or submarine bases are plotted because the reviewed sources did not identify any.
- Unverified projects and historical visits should not be interpreted as confirmation of current operational status.
